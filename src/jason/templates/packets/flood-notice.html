What the Association's policy covers

  • The whole building against flood, including the garages.
  • Inside each unit: walls, floors, ceilings, built-in cabinets, built-in appliances, the water heater, heating and air conditioning, fixtures, and improvements, up to the building limit.
  • Cleanup and debris removal, and some costs to protect the building when a flood is coming.

What it does not cover

  • Your belongings: furniture, clothing, electronics, and anything stored in your garage.
  • Washers and dryers, portable or window air conditioners, freezers, and carpet laid over a finished floor. Flood policies treat these as belongings.
  • Cars.
  • Temporary housing while repairs are made, and lost rent if you rent out your unit.
  • Damage from a sewer or drain backup when there is no flood in the area.

Your own coverage

The CC&Rs require each owner to insure their unit, upgrades, and belongings. A standard condominium owner's (HO-6) policy normally excludes flood, and a "water backup" endorsement is not flood insurance.

Please ask your insurance agent about a personal flood policy for your belongings. A new flood policy usually takes 30 days to start. Federal flood policies do not pay for temporary housing or lost rent; some private flood policies do. Also ask whether any policy you have would pay your share of an Association assessment after a flood. A federal (NFIP) owner policy does not pay an assessment for the Association's deductible.

For your lender

The enclosed declarations page is the proof of flood coverage most lenders ask for. Federal flood rules do not allow a unit owner's lender to be listed on the Association's flood policy, which is why the declarations page shows no mortgagee. If your lender also needs proof of the master policy, the enclosed Certificate of Insurance and the custom certificate instructions apply. If your lender asks for something else, contact the Association.

If a flood happens

  • Stay safe first.
  • Photograph the damage and the water line.
  • Report building damage to the Association right away. The Association files the building claim.
  • File any claim for your belongings with your own insurer, and keep your receipts.

Each building has its own flood policy, and each renews on its own date. Questions can be sent to {OFFICIAL_EMAIL}.
