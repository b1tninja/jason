## About the budget

The Association is a nonprofit corporation run by a volunteer board of directors elected from the owners. Each year's budget covers the coming year's operating costs, such as insurance, utilities, landscaping, repairs, and taxes, and saves in reserves for the future repair and replacement of the components the Association maintains. Questions about the budget are welcome by email or during open forum at any board meeting.
