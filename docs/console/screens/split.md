# Split document

`#/setup/split` (drafts and entry points), `#/setup/split/<id>` (one draft), `#/setup/split/demo` (a made-up draft with no server) in Setup · built (phases 2 and 3 of [../../pdf-splitter.md](../../pdf-splitter.md), except the model pass and the joins) · loaders `GET /api/split-sessions`, `GET /api/split-session?id=` (with `facts=`, `review=1`, and `flags=1`), pictures `GET /api/split/thumb?id=&page=&size=`, writer `POST /api/write/split/<id or new>` (`jason.web.extra.split`, `jason.web.split`), the library list `GET /api/library-files` and `POST /api/write/library/files` (`jason.web.extra.library_browse`) · design: [../../pdf-splitter.md](../../pdf-splitter.md), sections 3, 5, 7, 9.4 and 9.5

## In the console

`SplitView` (`ui/src/views/SplitView.tsx`) reads the hash: no id is the start screen, `demo` the demo, anything else a draft. Board only (officers, managers, administrators); not in `ownerScreens.json`, and the server refuses the owner view. A file's name is never in a route.

- **Start.** Open a PDF from three tabs, then **Check the file** (the dry run of `open`: pages, size, and the limit's words) and **Open it** (keeps a copy and a draft). *From this computer*: a file chooser, base64 up to `upload.max_bytes`. *From the library* (`LibraryBrowser.tsx`, [pdf-splitter.md](../../pdf-splitter.md) section 9.6): a search box (Enter or Search; the name goes in a POST body), chips for kind and for source with their counts (each says pressed with a check and a heavier outline, not by colour), a Text select, a Drafts select, a sort and a reverse button, and More filters (smallest page count, changed on or after, changed on or before); a table of name (a radio, so the arrow keys and a thumb choose), kind, pages, size, changed, text held or not, and a **draft exists** badge with a link that continues the draft; a confidential file is named by its kind and cannot be chosen outside the private view; a file this machine does not hold says so; 50 files at a time with **Show 50 more**; on the phone each file is a card. *From Drive*: **Choose a PDF in Drive** opens the record chooser's dialog (Drive and Paste a link only; folders browse, a PDF is a radio, any other type is listed off with why, no folder binding; Escape picks nothing); then Check says the file would be read once from Drive and that its pages are counted when it is read (a dry run downloads nothing). When Drive is not connected the screen says so with the command a person runs. Below, the drafts with their status in words (draft, confirmed, applied, stale, declined) and a link to each; a confidential file's label is masked as the server sent it.
- **Layout** (`SplitEditor.tsx`, `SplitDrawer.tsx`, `SplitMenus.tsx`, `splitLayout.ts`; design section 5.11). One column that fills the window: a title and summary line, a toolbar of menus (**Draft**, **View**, **Mark**, **Pages**, **Help**) with Undo, Redo, Segments, Compare, Focus and **Review and split**, a slim strip of numbered segment chips (a tap jumps to the segment), the pages, and the bottom bar (slider with ticks, Go to). The toolbar is one tab stop with the arrow keys; a menu opens with Enter, Space or Down and closes on Esc with the focus back on its button; a bulk act's item states its count (**Accept 11 at High confidence**, **Clear all my marks (3)…**). **Focus** (`F`) hides the console's header and menu so the pages fill the window; Esc leaves it. The segments, the compare pane and the review are **drawers** that overlay the pages from an edge (segments left or right, compare right, the review right or, on a phone, the bottom) and open from a toolbar button, a key (`S`, `C`), or an edge tab (a tap or a drag toward the pages); they close on Esc, Close, the grip (tap or drag back), or the scrim. From 1200 px a drawer can be **pinned**: it docks and the pages reflow. The segments drawer opens by itself, docked, from 1400 px and is closed under it. The choices (focus, drawers, side, pin, what is shown) are kept for the tab (`jason-split-layout-<id>`). The album's columns fill the width. A phone has a single View menu and a bottom action bar, and its drawers are full-width bottom sheets.
- **Views** (`1` to `4`, `V`): **Album** (windowed grid of 200 px pictures with a size slider), **Filmstrip** (the current page large, a strip of 96 px pictures, the slider), **Scroll** (one page at a time, down or across), **List** (the segments and open suggestions, windowed; the phone default). **Compare** opens as a drawer over any view (`C`, or a double click on a page): the page, the page before, and the first page of the segment before, with Previous page, Next page, the segment's first page, Fit, 800 px and an enlarged zoom, and Full screen. View also turns the suggestions, the flags and the rejected suggestions on and off.
- **Marking.** Tap or click toggles a page as the first page of a segment; Shift-click selects a range without marking; a long press (touch, 500 ms) or right click or `L` opens the page menu (start, nested start, remove, accept or reject, why, show larger, every Nth page or mark each or merge in a selection, reload the picture); the grip on a start drags it to another page (the grid scrolls at its edge); `[` and `]` move it a page. Page 1 is locked and says why.
- **Suggestions.** Dashed start with "Suggested, High" (a word and dash weight, never colour alone); the rail lists them with their reason; accept, reject, accept all at High, or at Medium or better (one undo step each); never automatic. Show rejected is a toggle.
- **Bulk.** In the Mark menu: Every Nth page (with the count first), split on blank pages (stay before, stay after, or leave out; the duplex warning when over a quarter are blank), split at page-number restarts, clear all my marks (a confirm). Each opens a small panel under the toolbar for its number or its plan; Esc or Close shuts it.
- **Saving.** A change shows at once and is sent in order with its version. The state is a word in the summary line: saving, saved with the time, offline (marks held on this device, retry, and sent when the browser is back online), not saved after a server error (kept, retry). A server refusal puts the page back and shows the server's words. A 409 shows both copies with **Keep mine**, **Keep theirs**, **Merge boundaries**. Undo and redo are the server's stack.
- **Review.** **Review and split** (enabled once saving has finished) opens the review: counts that must add up, where the new files go, the first page of each part (loaded only while on screen), a title field per part with the part's kind under it as a guess ("looks like: invoice, a guess", from the part's `kind`; hidden for a confidential file), the part's flags, the pages to turn, the pages to leave out (blank pages, a checkbox), collisions and duplicates in words. **Preview the split** is the dry run; the one confirm is labelled "Write N files as NAME". The result lists filled, taken in by the library, held (with the server's reason), skipped, failed, and the pages turned.
- **Flags** (`SplitFlags.tsx`; design section 9.5). The screen reads `flags=1` once the draft is saved, and again when more page facts have been measured. A segment can carry: **Blank only**, **Repeats segment N** or **Repeated by segment N** (the later segment names the earlier), **N sideways pages**, **May be over the size limit**, **Slot already filled**, and **May hold more than one document**. A flag is a glyph and words, never a colour alone, and a reading, not a decision: nothing is marked, moved, or written because of one. The rail row shows the first flag and a button ("+2 more") that opens every flag as a full sentence; the List view shows two. The dialog gives each flag its acts: **Go to page** for each sideways page and **Fix these N pages in the new files**; **Review these pages** for the pages that may start another document; **Go to segment N** for a repeat. The over-limit size is said to be an estimate ("about 4.9 MB (an estimate from the file's size in proportion to its pages)") until the review has cut the part and gives the exact size. The summary line says how many segments are flagged. A segment's flags are matched by its level and page range, so a flag is never shown against a segment the draft has since changed.
- **Review these pages.** A bar above the toolbar walks the listed pages one at a time (**Previous page to review**, **Next page to review**), says which one and why, and marks nothing until **Mark page N as a start** is pressed. It starts from the flags dialog, or from a part's flags in the review (which leaves the review for the pages).
- **Sideways pages.** From the page facts the server measured (`turn`, `sideways`, `fix`, `orientation`): a cell shows a turn glyph and the word "Sideways" (and, once a turn is chosen, the choice); its name and the page menu say "Sideways: its text is turned 90° clockwise; turning the page 270° clockwise makes it upright" and how the page is drawn. A scan with no text layer is never called sideways, and the page menu says why. The page menu offers **Fix sideways (turn N°)** for a page measured sideways, **Turn 90°, 180°, 270° clockwise** for any page (in the new files only), and **Do not turn this page**. The Pages menu has **Find and fix sideways pages…**, which opens a panel with **Find sideways pages** and **Fix all N sideways pages**. The choices are kept for the tab (`jason-split-turns-<id>`), like the pages to leave out.
- **Turning in the review.** **Pages to turn** lists every page measured sideways and every page chosen, each with a select (Leave as it is, Fix sideways, or a number of degrees), **Fix all N sideways pages**, and **Leave every page as it is**. The choices go as `rotate` (`{"61": 90, "66": "fix"}`) on `review` and `apply`. The review and the dry run say "N pages turned in the new files (...); M measured sideways stay as they are", each part says which of its pages it turns, and the result lists the turned pages. The original and the pictures on screen are never changed.
- **Filing in the review.** **Where the new files go** is **As each part is labelled** (the default, and the server's: a part with a slot fills it, any other is kept in the store), **Held**, **Library**, or **Slots**, sent as `filing` (absent for the default). A part can have its own choice (as chosen above, held, library, or its slot, offered only for a part that names one), sent in `parts` as `{ segment, filing }`. The counts say how many parts go where, how many are kept held because the slot is full, and how many are skipped as identical. A note says what Library does and does not do: the library intake reads the text layer and does not run OCR in the request, so a scan usually comes back kept in the store with the reason. The result shows what the server says and no more: parts taken in by the library (with kind and library id), parts kept with the reason, and "N parts were queued for OCR" only when the answer carries `ocrQueued` (a number or a list).
- **Magnifier** (`Loupe.tsx`, `splitLoupe.ts`; design section 5.12). A lens (220 px, rounded square) follows the pointer over any page picture (the album, scroll and filmstrip cells and the compare figure) and shows that spot at 3 times the cell, from 2 to 6 (**View > Magnifier zoom**, or `Alt` and the wheel; the plain wheel still scrolls). The cell's own picture is shown at once, upscaled; after 150 ms on a page the sharp one is swapped in place (the server's 1600 px, or, for a file chosen on this computer, the page drawn again by the browser up to 300 dpi and 3300 px). It never takes a click: marking, shift-click and drag of a start work through it. On or off from the **Magnifier** toolbar button (pressed state), **View > Magnifier (on / off)** (a check and a word), or `E` (`M` stays the merge key); on by default with a mouse or trackpad that hovers, off on touch, kept per tab. With the keyboard, the focused page shows the lens beside it; `Alt` and the arrows move the spot, `Alt` and `+` `-` zoom, `Esc` hides it. On touch, with the **Magnifier** button on (the phone's bottom bar): press and hold a page about 350 ms without moving, then drag; the lens rides above the finger; lifting marks nothing; the long-press page menu is off in that mode (use the Pages menu or `L`). The lens is `role="img"` named "Magnified page N"; changes of state and zoom are announced politely.
- **Keys.** Arrows, Space and Enter, `N` `P`, `A` `X`, `M`, `[` `]`, `L`, `G` or `/`, `V`, `1`-`4`, `+` `-`, `F` (focus), `E` (magnifier), `Alt`+arrows and `Alt`+`+` `-` (magnifier), `S` (segments drawer), `C` (compare drawer), `Z` `Shift+Z` `Y` `Ctrl+Z`, `?`, `Esc`, Home, End, Page Up and Down. The layout keys (`F`, `E`, `S`, `C`, `?`, `Esc`) work from the toolbar and from a drawer too; the page keys do not. Esc clears the selection, then closes the tool panel, a drawer that overlays, and last leaves focus mode.

## Pictures: where they come from, and what size

- **A file chosen on this computer** opens the editor at once (`#/setup/split/local`), drawn by the browser from the file ("Preview from this computer" under the counts). The server's copy is made in the background; a notice says so, marks wait in order, and when the draft exists its id replaces `local` in the address. A refusal from the server is shown in its words.
- **A library or Drive file** is drawn by the server: `/api/split/thumb`, windowed and prioritised. The PDF is not sent to the browser.
- **Size follows the cell.** The slider enlarges cells and the picture is replaced in place by a sharper one (96, 200, 400, 800, 1600 px) once the size settles; the memory budget caps the step. Compare "Enlarged" asks for the sharpest.
- **Each cell says "Measuring…"** until the server's measures for it arrive; blank, sideways, repeats, page-number runs, flags and suggestions appear as they land.
- If the browser cannot be trusted to draw (a failed self-test of JPEG 2000 or CCITT, the file too long, browser drawing off for the community), the line under the counts says so and the server draws.

## Performance

**Bands (the album).** Each top-level document has a numbered header row before its pages: "Document N", "starts at page P", the page count, and the title the server has for its first page (a guess until confirmed; hidden for a confidential file). A document's pages start on a new row, so a band never shares a row with another document's page. Bands are fixed-height rows (30 px), so there is still no measuring: `bandedLayout` builds the row offsets once per layout as prefix sums (a `Float64Array`), and the window, a page's row, the scroll to a page, a jump to a segment (`GridApi.scrollToSegment`), the page at the middle of the view and the arrow-key row step (`rowDelta`, which skips bands and keeps the column) are binary searches over them. At the worst, 3,000 one-page documents (6,000 rows), the build is under a millisecond and the window search about half a microsecond per scroll event (measured in `splitwindow.test.ts` under Node; not a browser frame time). Every second band is hatched as well as tinted, and the number is words. The scroll, filmstrip and list views have no bands. A re-banding (marking a start) moves cells to other rows, which are new elements, so the grid gives the focus back to the cell that had it. A suggestion is drawn in the accent, not the warning colour (the attention ladder gives warning to a clock): a dashed border, the tag "Suggested, High" (or Medium or Low), and heavier dashes at High and dots at Low; a start is the solid bar.

Cells have a fixed size, so the scroll length is arithmetic (`splitWindow.ts`); only the rows in view plus two screens either side exist (about 30 to 60 cells for a 3,000-page file). No cell holds React state: one set of handlers sits on the grid, a cell is memoised on primitives, and a picture arrives by the loader setting `img.src` (`splitThumbs.ts`), so a load costs no render. The loader asks nearest the view first and ahead of the scroll, holds six requests, aborts a request when its cell leaves, in a fast scroll asks for 96 px only and asks for the rest 120 ms after the scroll settles, and keeps at most 150 tiny, 60 small, 30 medium, 4 large and 2 huge pictures (half on a phone or a device with 2 GB or less), never dropping one on screen. A placeholder is drawn from the page's 16 by 16 facts picture as a BMP data URL, with no canvas. Facts come in chunks of 300 as the window reaches them.

## Try it with no server

`#/setup/split/demo` is a made-up 120-page draft answered from memory (`splitDemo.ts`): the same acts, words and refusals as the server, pictures drawn as SVG, suggestions from the same kinds of signals. `#/setup/split/demo?pages=3000` makes a long one to feel the scrolling. Reload to start over. Nothing is sent anywhere. The demo answers the flags, the sideways facts, `rotate` and `filing` as the server does, on made-up data: pages 19 (a map drawn landscape, text turned 90°), 61 and 66 are measured sideways and drawn turned, and the scanned pages are never measured; the whole file is over a made-up 2 MB limit; the segments that start at pages 10, 20 and 74 have a made-up slot, and the one at 20 is full, so its part is kept held; pages 72 to 73 repeat pages 45 to 46 once each is its own segment; and the made-up library intake files a part that starts on a titled page and keeps any other with "no kind read". Mark pages 10 and 20 and open the review to try the Filing choices; mark pages 10 and 11 and choose Library to see a part kept with its reason.

## Not built here

The model pass, the joins from the library menu and record slots (a part's slot is chosen on the slot page; the splitter only chooses where the files go), a library part's OCR (the apply does not queue it, so the screen says nothing about it unless the server does), the vector compare pane, the marquee and pinch gestures, the 1,600 px picture, "Remove page pictures" (no route), and a live save when two people edit. Section 9 of the design lists them.
