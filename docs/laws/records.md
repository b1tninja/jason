# Association records and annual reports

Civil Code §§ 5200–5240, 5260, and 5300–5320, from the 2025 session publication. The kinds of association record are modeled as `AssociationRecord`. A Drive file is pinned as one of those records when its text matches. A map, condominium plan, common-area deed, public report, or plan set is pinned with `deliver()` when it is a document the subdivider had to provide. `community.developer_file()` is that inventory. An empty tuple is a document the shelf does not yet have.

## What counts

Section 5200(a) is the list. Enhanced records (invoices, statements, canceled checks, reimbursement requests) and election materials are inside that list by paragraphs (a)(13) and (a)(14). Interim statements in paragraph (a)(3) are prepared on an accrual or modified accrual basis. Election materials are kept for one year after the election.

Executive-session minutes are not the minutes members inspect under paragraph (a)(8). The next open minutes generally note that an executive session occurred (§ 4935).

## Produce them

| Request | Deadline | Section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A record from the current fiscal year | 10 business days | § 5210(b)(1) |
| A record from the prior two fiscal years | 30 calendar days | § 5210(b)(2) |
| Minutes of a member or board meeting | The time in § 4950: available within 30 days of the meeting | §§ 4950, 5210 |
| A record already covered by the escrow, annual-report, reserve-summary, or insurance-notice statutes | The deadline in that statute | § 5210(b)(3) |

Inspection is at the association’s business office in the development, or at an agreed place, or by individual delivery of copies if they cannot agree or the member asks for copies (§ 5205). The member may name a representative in writing. Copies can go out electronically if the redacted file cannot be altered. Copying and mailing are at actual cost, and the member agrees to the cost first. Redacting an enhanced record may be billed up to $10 an hour and $200 a request.

If the association withholds or redacts and the member asks why, the association states the legal basis in writing (§ 5215(d)). Compensation of employees is not withheld, and it is reported by job title, not by name.

## Annual budget report (§ 5300)

Distribute it 30 to 90 days before the fiscal year ends. It includes, unless the governing documents are stricter:

- A pro forma operating budget on an accrual basis
- A reserve summary, and the full reserve plan on request
- A statement of any outstanding loan, and whether a special assessment is anticipated
- A summary of insurance: insurer, type, limit, deductible, plus the bold § 5300 warning that the summary is not the policy
- The FHA certification statement, on its own page, when that paragraph applies

Section 5320 lets the association deliver the full report or a summary that says the full report is available. A member who asked for full reports receives the full report.

## Annual policy statement (§ 5310)

The board distributes it in the same 30-to-90-day window. It includes the address for official communications (§ 4035), and the other items that section lists, including where to get minutes (§ 4950) and the penalty schedule if the association fines members (§ 5850).

## Reviewed financial statement (§ 5305)

If gross income exceeds $75,000, a California-licensed accountant reviews the financial statement under generally accepted accounting principles, and the association delivers it within 120 days after the fiscal year closes.

## Membership list

Section 5200(a)(9) is the membership list. Section 5240 also gives members access to membership lists through Corporations Code § 8330. The list the association keeps is the Membership workbook. A county grant deed is evidence of title. It is not a substitute for that list: the grantee may be a trust, a company, or someone who has since transferred.

A member who wants the list states a purpose reasonably related to the member’s interest as a member (§ 5225). If the association denies the request and the member sues under § 5235, the association has the burden on that point. Jason can assemble the list with opt-outs removed. Jason does not decide the purpose and does not deliver the list.

| Rule | What it requires | Section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opt out | A member may keep name and address off the list given to another member. The opt-out lasts until the member changes it | § 5220 |
| Change the list | A change of list information is in writing, delivered under § 4035 | § 5260 |
| Do not sell | The association and its managing agent do not sell a member’s personal information without consent | § 5230 |
| Safe at Home | Use the Secretary of State substitute address, and keep participation confidential | § 5216 |
| Withholding | If the association withholds or redacts and the member asks, it states the legal basis in writing | § 5215(d) |
| Deadline | Current fiscal year, 10 business days. The prior two fiscal years, 30 calendar days | § 5210 |

An association mailing to its own members is not this request. The association may use the names to send its own notices. That mailing is one envelope per destination, with every owner at that address in the name block and no one marked primary. A member’s copy of the list leaves off § 5220 opt-outs. The association’s own mailing does not.

## Other records a member may ask for

The same article covers the rest of § 5200, not only the list. Jason identifies the record and where it is pinned. Jason does not decide the request.

| Record | Where Jason looks | Section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governing documents | `GoverningDocument`, pins, the Drive governing-document folders | § 5200(a)(11) |
| Minutes | Meeting folders and the minutes known file. Not executive-session minutes | § 5200(a)(8), § 4950 |
| Financial disclosures, interim statements, reserves, tax returns, check register | Financials library. A treasurer’s report is the interim statement | § 5200(a)(1), (a)(3), (a)(6), (a)(7), (a)(10) |

A treasurer’s report is filed twice. The redacted copy a member can receive is in PayHOA `Financials/{year}/` and in the Drive Financials folder. The complete copy is in `Confidential/Complete Financial Statements/{year}/`. Redacted copies also sit in `Resale Documents/` for escrow. `Email Attachments/` is the inbox, not the shelf. The 2023 monthly packets are named `Financial Statements`, not treasurer’s reports, so they stay unclassified.
| Contracts | Contracts, leases, and insurance policies pinned as executed contracts | § 5200(a)(4) |
| Election materials | Elections folder. Kept one year. Signed envelopes may be inspected and not copied | § 5200(c) |
| Exterior elevated element inspection reports | The latest § 5551 report, with the reserve study. Sellers also give that report to a buyer. Kept for two inspection cycles | § 5200(a)(15), § 5210(a)(3), § 5551(i), § 4525(a)(11) |

Inspection is at the association’s business office in the development, or at a place the member and the association agree, or by copies if they cannot agree or the member asks for copies (§ 5205). The member may name a representative in writing. Copying and mailing are at actual cost, and the member agrees to that cost first. Electronic copies are allowed when the redacted file cannot be altered. A court that finds access unreasonably withheld may award the member’s costs and a civil penalty of up to $500 for each denied written request (§ 5235).
