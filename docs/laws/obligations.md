# What the association must do

Duties below are ones the 2025 Civil Code text places on the association (or on its board or manager). A duty that says “unless the governing documents require more” still has to be checked against the CC&Rs and bylaws. Jason can identify the duty and the file that would satisfy it. Jason does not lien, foreclose, or impose discipline.

## Keep

| Duty | Section | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep association records and make them available for inspection and copying | §§ 5205, 5210 | The kinds in § 5200. Current fiscal year and the two before it, except minutes, which stay available |
| Make board minutes (or a draft or summary) available within 30 days | § 4950 | Not executive-session minutes. Distribute a copy on request, at cost |
| Note executive-session matters, in general, in the next open meeting’s minutes | § 4935 | A general note, not the confidential discussion |
| Keep election materials for one year after the election | § 5200(c) | Ballots, voter list, proxies, candidate list, tally. Signed envelopes may be inspected and not copied |
| Keep a separate record of association funds the manager receives | § 5380(c) | Receipt and disposition, including interest |
| Keep Safe at Home participation confidential, and use the Secretary of State substitute address | § 5216 | On request of a participant |
| Prepare interim financial statements on an accrual or modified accrual basis | § 5200(a)(3) | Balance sheet, income and expense, budget comparison, general ledger |

## Distribute or publish

| Duty | Section | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual budget report, by individual delivery, or a summary with the full report available | §§ 5300, 5320 | 30 to 90 days before the fiscal year ends |
| Annual policy statement | §§ 5310, 5320 | Same window |
| Reviewed financial statement, if gross income exceeds $75,000 | § 5305 | Within 120 days after the fiscal year closes, by individual delivery |
| Schedule of monetary penalties, in the annual policy statement, if the association imposes penalties | § 5850 | Also give the latest schedule to a member on request |
| Notice of a board meeting, with the agenda, by general delivery | § 4920 | At least 4 days; 2 days for a meeting held only in executive session. Follow the governing documents if they require longer |
| How to get minutes, inside the annual policy statement | § 4950(b) | With the policy statement |
| Insurance nonrenewal, if replacement coverage will not be in place when the policy lapses | § 5810 | Immediately, to the members |
| Escrow disclosures the contract assigns to the manager | §§ 4530, 5376 | The manager facilitates delivery when the contract says the manager delivers them |
| Fine schedule and member-discipline notice | §§ 5850, 5855 | Hearing notice at least 10 days ahead; written decision within 14 days |

The annual budget report’s insurance summary names the insurer, the type, the limit, and the deductible, and it carries the § 5300 warning that the summary is not the policy (§ 5300).

## Meet

| Duty | Section |
| --- | --- |
| Take board action only at a board meeting. Do not meet by a chain of email | § 4910 |
| Let members attend open meetings and speak, within a time limit the board sets | §§ 4925, 5000 |
| Hold member meetings under a recognized or adopted parliamentary procedure | § 5000 |
| Adjourn to executive session for member discipline (if the member asks), a payment plan, or a decision to foreclose | § 4935 |

## Money, reserves, insurance

| Duty | Section |
| --- | --- |
| The board reviews the association’s finances on the schedule in §§ 5500 and 5501 | §§ 5500, 5501 |
| Do not transfer reserve or operating funds above the § 5380 threshold without prior written board approval | § 5380 |
| Use reserve funds only as § 5510 allows. A temporary transfer follows § 5515. The reserve-study guide is [reserves.md](reserves.md) | §§ 5510, 5515 |

## Written requests the association must honor as written

Civil Code § 5260: a change of membership-list information, a second mailing or email address, individual delivery of general notices, a membership-list opt-out, a request for the full annual reports, and an opt-in or opt-out of electronic secret ballot. Each one has to be in writing, delivered under § 4035.

## Limits, not chores

These sections restrict the association more than they assign a document to produce. Do not treat them as a publication calendar.

- Do not grant a member exclusive use of common area without the member vote § 4600 requires, unless an exception in that section applies. A measure put to the members states whether the association is paid and who insures the exclusive use.
- Do not prohibit the protected uses in §§ 4705–4753 (flag, signs, pets, rentals, EV charging, solar, drought landscaping, and the rest of that article) beyond what the section allows. An electric-vehicle or roof-solar installation that needs exclusive use of common area is also a § 4600 question.
- Decide an architectural application within the time § 4765 states.
- Do not sell a member’s personal information, or transmit it, without consent, unless the law requires it (§ 5230).
- Assessment collection has its own notice sequence (§§ 5660–5730). Identifying a delinquent account is in scope. Recording a lien or foreclosing is not a Jason task.
