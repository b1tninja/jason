# What Jason can assist with

This page applies the duty anchors in [community-manager.md](../community-manager.md) to the jobs Jason already does. The statutory index is [obligations.md](obligations.md), [records.md](records.md), and [bpc.md](bpc.md). It is not legal advice. Jason identifies the duty and prepares the artifact. The board decides.

## Identify, then stop

These duties have a statute and an artifact. Jason can name the section and the file. Jason does not perform the act.

| Duty | What to identify | Stop before |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meetings | The minutes pin and the Meetings folder. Executive-session minutes are not that pin | Writing the board’s decision |
| Elections | Election rules as governing documents. Ballots are election materials, kept one year | Running the election or copying a signed envelope |
| Architecture and discipline | The request, the governing-document passage, the hearing notice periods in §§ 4765 and 5855 | Approving, denying, or imposing the penalty |
| Exclusive use | Whether the request would grant exclusive use of common area, and whether § 4600(a) or an exception in § 4600(b) applies | Granting that exclusive use, or treating an architectural approval as the grant |
| Manager disclosures | §§ 5375, 5375.5, and 11504, and whether fidelity coverage includes the agent (§ 5806) | Signing the management contract |
| Trust account | § 5380 when the manager holds funds the association does not control | Moving money |

## How a task starts

1. Name the duty anchor.
2. Read the section from the current publication. A 2011 index hit is not that text. `CIV 4000` and `CIV 5200` are `not_in_index` there.
3. Open the association's shelf or the catalog row named on this page.
4. Leave the decision with the board.
