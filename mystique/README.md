# Mystique community specification

This package is the Mystique Community Association. `Mystique` is a class. Buildings, transaction rules, Drive anchors, PayHOA folders, site pages, and document sync rules are objects on that class. So are the shared solar program (`solar.py`), the superseded annexations (`annexations.py`), the floor plans (`plans.py`), and the utility agencies' tax-roll codes (`utilities.py`): which direct charge on a Mystique tax bill carries a delinquent account the city or the sewer district first recorded as a lien.

Jason’s helpers live in `jason.community`. They classify. They do not hold these facts.

Secrets stay in `.env` and Keeper.
