## 9. Dispute resolution (§5310(a)(9), §5920, §5965)

**Internal dispute resolution (Civil Code §§5900 to 5920).** An owner or the Association may ask, in writing, to meet and confer about a dispute over rights, duties, or liabilities under the Davis-Stirling Act, the Nonprofit Mutual Benefit Corporation Law, or the governing documents. The board designates a director to meet with the owner, the Association charges no fee, and a written resolution signed by both sides is binding if it is consistent with the law and the governing documents.

**Alternative dispute resolution (Civil Code §§5925 to 5965, Article 3).** Before filing certain lawsuits to enforce the governing documents or the Davis-Stirling Act, an owner or the Association must offer the other side alternative dispute resolution, such as mediation or arbitration, as Article 3 describes.
