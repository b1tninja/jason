# What the Bylaws say about the officers, and what is an officer's alone

A reading of Article 10 of the Bylaws, with the sections that bear on it (8.3, 8.6, 8.14, 8.17, 9.1, 9.6, 7.11, 12.9, 12.10), to settle which actions jason treats as an officer's alone ([../../docs/console/screens/office-views.md](../../docs/console/screens/office-views.md), decision 5). Each provision is quoted whole from jason's stored copy (`jason cite "Bylaws 10.8"`), then read. A reading is marked as one. The stored copy is not an official restatement: the recorded and adopted Bylaws control.

## The words

**10.1 Enumeration of Officers.** "The officers of the Association shall be a President, a Vice-President, a Secretary, and a Chief Financial Officer (who may from time to time be referred to as the Treasurer), who shall at all times be members of the Board of Directors, and such other officers as the Board of Directors may, from time to time, by resolution appoint."

**10.2 Election of Officers.** "The Board of Directors shall elect the officers. The election of officers shall take place immediately following the election of the Board of Directors, or at the first meeting of the Board of Directors following each annual meeting of the Members."

**10.3 Term.** "The officers of this Association shall be elected annually by the Board, and each shall hold office for one (1) year, unless he or she shall sooner resign, be removed by the Board, or otherwise be disqualified to serve."

**10.4 Special Appointments.** "The Board may elect such other officers as the affairs of the Association may require, each of whom shall hold office for such period, have such authority, and perform such duties as the Board may, from time to time, determine."

**10.5 Resignation and Removal.** "Any officer may be removed from office, with or without cause, by the Board. Any officer may resign at any time by giving written notice to the Board, the President, or the Secretary. ..."

**10.6 Vacancies.** "A vacancy in any office may be filled by appointment by the Board. ..."

**10.7 Multiple Offices.** "The offices of Secretary and Treasurer may be held by the same person. No person shall simultaneously hold more than one of any of the other offices, except in the case of special offices created pursuant to Section 10.4 of these Bylaws."

**10.8 President.** "The President shall be the chief executive officer of the Association and shall, subject to control of the Board of Directors, have general supervision, direction, and control of the affairs and the other officers and the employees and agents of the Association. The President shall preside at all meetings of the Members and at all meetings of the Board of Directors, shall have the general powers and duties of management usually vested in the office of the President of an Association, and shall have such other powers and duties as may be prescribed by the Board of Directors and the Bylaws, subject, however, to any limitations contained in the Declaration."

**10.9 Vice-President.** "In the absence or disability of the President, the Vice-President shall perform all the duties of the President, and when so acting, shall have all of the powers of, and be subject to all of the restrictions upon, the President. The Vice-President shall have such other powers and perform such other duties as, from time to time, may be prescribed by the Board of Directors."

**10.10 Secretary.** "The Secretary shall keep or cause to be kept, at the principal office or such other place as the Board of Directors may prescribe, a book of minutes of all meetings of Directors, Members, and Committees of the Board ... and all the proceedings thereof. The Secretary shall give, or cause to be given, notice of all meetings of the Members and of the Board of Directors required by the Bylaws or by law to be given and shall maintain a proper record of the giving of such notice, and shall keep the books, records, and documents of the Association in safe custody, and shall have such other powers and perform such other duties as may be prescribed by the Board of Directors or the Bylaws." (The ellipsis omits the list of what the minutes set forth: time and place, how a special meeting was authorized, the notice, those present, the votes present, and the proceedings.)

**10.11 Treasurer.** "The Treasurer shall be responsible for the receipt and deposit in appropriate accounts of all monies of the Association and shall cause disbursement of such funds as directed by resolution of the Board of Directors; may sign all checks and promissory notes of the Association; shall cause to be kept proper books of account; shall cause an annual review of the Association's books and financial statements to be made by a public accountant at the completion of any fiscal year for which such review is required by law or as determined by the Board; shall assist the Board in preparation of an annual budget and a statement of income and expenditures to be presented to the Members of the Association as provided by law; and shall have such other powers and perform such other duties as may be prescribed by the Board of Directors."

**12.9 Checks, Drafts, and Evidences of Indebtedness.** "All checks, drafts, or other orders for payment of money and all notes or other evidences of indebtedness, issued in the name of the Association shall be signed by (i) any two Directors, (ii) any two officers of the Association, (iii) any officer of the Association and any Director, or (iv) in any other manner specified by the Board of Directors; provided, however, that the signatures of at least two persons who shall be members of the Board of Directors or one member of the Board of Directors and one officer who is not a member of the Board of Directors shall be required for the withdrawal of funds from the Association's reserve account."

**8.3 Contracts.** "Subject to the limitations on contracts described in Section 8.17 below, the Board shall have the power to authorize any officer or officers to enter into any contract in the name of, or on behalf of, the Association. Unless expressly authorized by resolution of the Board, no officer shall have any power or authority to bind the Association or to render the Association liable for any purpose or on any account."

**8.6 Manager.** "The Board shall have the power to engage the services of a manager or management company as either an employee or an independent contractor, and engage such other employees or independent contractors as the Board may deem necessary, and to prescribe their duties."

**8.14 Bank Accounts.** "The Board shall have the power to open bank accounts and designate signatories upon such bank accounts, subject to any restrictions set forth in the Governing Documents."

**9.1 Records and Minutes.** "The Board of Directors shall cause to be kept a complete record of all its acts and the corporate affairs, including an accurate and current record of the Members setting forth their names and addresses, adequate and correct books and records of account, and minutes of the proceedings of the Members, the Board, and Committees of the Board, and to present a statement thereof to the Members at the annual meeting of the Members."

**9.6 Review of Accounts.** "The Board of Directors shall review the Association's operating and reserve accounts at least in accordance with the following minimum requirements: ..." (quarterly reconciliations, the reserve compared to the budget, the latest account statements, and an income and expense statement). Civil Code 5500 requires the same reviews monthly "unless the governing documents impose more stringent standards"; the Bylaws' quarterly minimum is not more stringent. This is the existing Conflict row `bylaws-quarterly-review`.

## The reading

**1. Every officer is a director.** 10.1 says the officers "shall at all times be members of the Board of Directors." Reading: there is no officer who is not a director, so "directors and officers" is one body, and a board member's information rights are the same whatever the office. This matches the board's decision of 2026-10-11.

**2. The board acts; an officer acts only as the board authorizes.** 8.3 gives an officer no power to bind the Association "unless expressly authorized by resolution of the Board." 10.8, 10.9, 10.10, and 10.11 each end with "such other powers and ... duties as may be prescribed by the Board." Reading: apart from the duties the Bylaws name below, an act is the board's, and any director may take part in it; an officer's own act needs a resolution.

**3. What the Bylaws name as an office's own duty.**

| Office | The duty the Bylaws name | Reading for jason |
|---|---|---|
| President | Presides at all meetings of the Members and of the Board; general supervision of the affairs, the other officers, the employees, and the agents, subject to the board's control | Chairing a meeting is the President's. Supervising the manager is the President's, under the board |
| Vice-President | Does all of the President's duties, with all the President's powers, "in the absence or disability of the President" | The President's actions open to the Vice-President only while the President is absent or disabled. jason cannot tell either: a person records it (a proposal: the secretary notes it in the minutes) |
| Secretary | Keeps the minutes book of the Board, the Members, and the committees; gives or causes to be given notice of every meeting required by the Bylaws or law, and keeps the record of giving it; keeps the books, records, and documents in safe custody | Recording the board's decisions and the minutes, recording that a notice was given, and keeping the records are the Secretary's. "Keep or cause to be kept" and "give, or cause to be given" let the Secretary delegate the work, not the duty |
| Treasurer | Responsible for the receipt and deposit of all monies; causes disbursement as the board directs by resolution; may sign checks and notes; causes the books to be kept; causes the annual review by a public accountant; assists the board with the budget and the statement of income and expenditures | The books, the deposits, and the disbursements as directed are the Treasurer's responsibility ("shall cause" allows the manager or a bookkeeper to do the work). The budget is the board's, with the Treasurer's assistance |

**4. Signing is not an officer's alone.** 10.11 says the Treasurer "may sign all checks." 12.9 lets "any two Directors," "any two officers," or "any officer ... and any Director" sign, and reserve withdrawals need two board members. Reading: a second signature on a payment is not the treasurer's exclusive act; any two directors may sign, and the Bylaws leave the board free to specify another manner. The profile's `approves` (`mystique/officers.py`) governs the drafts jason writes for a person to post or send, a different thing from signing a check. Which two signers a given bank account honors is the board's designation (8.14) and the bank's records, not jason's.

**5. The monthly review is the board's.** Bylaws 9.6 gives the review to "the Board of Directors," and Civil Code 5500 says "the board shall do all of the following." Neither names the Treasurer. Reading: every director may record that they reviewed; the Treasurer causes the documents to be ready. The monthly-review screen already allows each director to record their own review.

**6. The manager's authority is whatever the board prescribes.** 8.6 gives the board the power "to prescribe their duties." The Bylaws state no spending or repair limit for a manager. Reading: the manager's limit lives in the board's resolution or the management agreement, not in the Bylaws (the open decision 9 of the workflows README).

**7. Limits no officer can pass.** 8.17 bars the board, and so every officer, from contracts for more than one year (with listed exceptions), capital improvements over five percent of budgeted gross expenses in a fiscal year, selling property over five percent, paying directors or officers compensation, filling a vacancy caused by the members' removal of a director, and entering a unit in a non-emergency without 24 hours' written notice, unless the members consent as that section says.

## What this means for the screens

- **Officer-only actions** (decision 5 of the office-views spec), by this reading: presiding at a meeting (President, or the Vice-President while the President is absent or disabled); recording the board's decisions, the minutes, and that a notice was given (the Secretary); and answering for the books, deposits, and disbursements (the Treasurer, who may delegate the doing). Everything else is the board's act, and any director may take part in it.
- **A second signature on a money plan** is not treasurer-only. Any two directors, or an officer and a director, may sign. jason's per-person `approves` should list who the board has designated for each account (8.14), not the Treasurer by default.
- **The Vice-President's actions** are live only while a person has recorded that the President is absent or disabled.
- **Secretary and Treasurer held by one person** is allowed (10.7); no one may hold two of the other offices. The roster already holds such a person as two rows.

## Questions this does not settle

- Whether the Bylaws' "any two Directors" permits the second signature on every plan kind, including the reserve (12.9 sets a two-board-member floor for reserve withdrawals).
- How a President's "absence or disability" is recorded; the Bylaws name no procedure.
- Whether the board has by resolution prescribed other duties for any office (10.8 to 10.11 and 10.4 contemplate it). jason has no list of such resolutions; the minutes would show them.
- ~~Whether "the Chief Financial Officer" and "Treasurer" are one office~~: settled 2026-10-11. The Chief Financial Officer is the Treasurer, and the chief executive officer is the President (10.1, 10.8).

What the board might add as safeguards, drawn from these provisions, is in [controls.md](controls.md).
