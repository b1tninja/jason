# Variance request

Status: design (2026-10-05). Key `variance-request`; proposed marker code `VR`. It follows the standard in [form-templates.md](../../../docs/form-templates.md). The inventory row is "Variance" in [standard-forms.md](../standard-forms.md) (the worked example; `ResponseKind.VARIANCE`, “no form”); the general family is [docs/standard-forms.md](../../../docs/standard-forms.md) (family two: the documents make the owner ask). This is the association’s page, so it names the association’s sections. **Not held:** nothing in the documents or in the board’s hold bars this form; it is designed, and offered when the board adopts it and a person builds it.

## 1. Authority and what the law requires

As of: the association’s documents as jason holds them (`data/governing/ccrs.md`, `data/artifacts/site-docs/governing_documents/`), read 2026-10-05, and the shelf’s 2025 session publication of the code (`CIV-4900-4955.md`, `CIV-4760-4766.md`), read the same day. The words below are recited from jason’s copy and labeled with where they are written. A document kept as amended is consolidated from the instruments’ own words; it is not an official restatement, and the recorded and adopted documents control.

**The instrument.** The Restated Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions for the association, recorded September 20, 2007, Sacramento County Document No. 200709200938. Its Section 4.20 is “Variances”. Neither the First, the Second, nor the Third Amendment touches 4.20 (the Second Amendment amends 4.15; the Third amends 7.1 and 7.4). The three procedure steps below are printed as bullets in jason’s copy; the profile’s inventory letters them 4.20(a) (Initial Board Determination), 4.20(b) (Board Hearings), and 4.20(c) (Board Decision).

**CC&Rs 4.20, Variances** (the power):

> The Board shall be authorized to grant reasonable variances from the provisions of Article 4 of this Declaration upon written application from any Owner provided that the Board determines, in its sole discretion, that the specific application of the restriction to such Owner will
>
> cause substantial undue hardship to the Owner, or
>
> fail to further or accomplish the common plan for the Development as contemplated by this Declaration. The Board shall have the power to limit any variance granted in scope or duration or otherwise impose such specific requirements as the Board may, in its complete discretion, see fit to require. The Board shall follow the following procedures in acting on any request for a variance:

**CC&Rs 4.20(a), Initial Board Determination:**

> Initial Board Determination. The Board, in its sole discretion, shall make an initial determination whether or not the variance on its face meets the requirements set forth in this section. If the Board determines that it does not, the variance request shall be denied and the Board shall so notify the applicant within thirty days of the Board’s decision. If the Board determines that the variance does, the procedures set forth in the remainder of this section shall be followed.

**CC&Rs 4.20(b), Board Hearings:**

> Board Hearings. The Board shall conduct a hearing on the variance within forty-five days of the receipt of the written request for a variance. Notice shall be given to all Members not less than fifteen days prior to the date of the hearing. Members may submit comments in writing prior to the hearing and/or appear at the hearing. The Board shall establish a reasonable time limit for Member comments during the hearing. No decision regarding the request for variance shall be made until the conclusion of the hearing.

**CC&Rs 4.20(c), Board Decision:**

> Board Decision. After the conclusion of the hearing, the Board shall, in its sole discretion, either grant or deny the request for variance in accordance with the standards set forth in this section. As more fully discussed above, if the Board grants the variance request, the Board may impose such conditions as the Board deems appropriate and shall so notify the applicant within thirty (30) days of the Board’s decision.

**CIV 4765(a)(2), (4), and (5)** (the architectural procedure; it applies if the variance is a “physical change to the member’s separate interest or to the common area” and the governing documents require approval, as CC&Rs 5.1 does):

> (2) A decision on a proposed change shall be made in good faith and may not be unreasonable, arbitrary, or capricious.
>
> (4) A decision on a proposed change shall be in writing. If a proposed change is disapproved, the written decision shall include both an explanation of why the proposed change is disapproved and a description of the procedure for reconsideration of the decision by the board.
>
> (5) If a proposed change is disapproved, the applicant is entitled to reconsideration by the board, at an open meeting of the board. This paragraph does not require reconsideration of a decision that is made by the board or a body that has the same membership as the board, at a meeting that satisfies the requirements of Article 2 (commencing with Section 4900) of Chapter 6. Reconsideration by the board does not constitute dispute resolution within the meaning of Section 5905.

**CIV 4920(a), (c), (d)** (a hearing held as a board meeting; [meeting-comment-request.md](../../../docs/form-templates/meeting-comment-request.md) quotes them): notice “at least four days before the meeting”; given “by general delivery pursuant to Section 4045”; containing “the agenda for the meeting”. The 4.20(b) fifteen days are longer and control as to the hearing’s notice.

**CIV 4925(b)** (the board’s time limit for members who speak): “The board shall permit any member to speak at any meeting of the association or the board, except for meetings of the board held in executive session. A reasonable time limit for all members of the association to speak to the board or before a meeting of the association shall be established by the board.”

**What the documents do not say** (so the form does not say it either): a time within which the Board makes the initial determination; a time within which the Board notifies the applicant of a denial after the hearing (4.20(c) states the thirty days for a grant, with conditions); what the notice to all Members says or how it is given; whether the applicant may be reconsidered; whether the decision is final; a fee for the application or the hearing.

## 2. Who uses it, and when

- **An Owner** (a member) who asks to be excused from a restriction in Article 4 of the Declaration because the restriction would cause “substantial undue hardship” or would “fail to further or accomplish the common plan”. 4.20: “upon written application from any Owner”.
- **When:** at any time. The documents set no season and no deadline for an application.
- **What it covers:** only “the provisions of Article 4” (use restrictions: residential use, businesses, nuisances, signs, antennas, trash, vehicles and parking, garages, animals, rental of condominiums, and the others in that article). A restriction elsewhere (Article 5’s approval of exterior modifications, Article 7’s maintenance duties) is not within 4.20; the form says so.
- **Who it is not for:** a tenant or resident (the application is the Owner’s); an owner who wants to waive 4.15 for hardship or a limited term (the specific route is 4.15(c): [rental-application.md](rental-application.md), held); an owner asking for approval of an exterior change (CC&Rs 5.1 and B-18); an owner asking for a longer guest-parking stay (4.11(e): the Board “may, in its discretion, permit” it, with no hearing); an owner asking for a change because of a disability ([the accommodation page](../../../docs/form-templates/accommodation-request.md)).

## 6. What the member is told

The sentence the Owner reads (`member_clock`):

> The Board will decide whether your request, on its face, meets the requirements for a variance. If it does not, the Board will deny it and tell you in writing within thirty days of its decision. If it does, the Board will hold a hearing within forty-five days after we received your request, and every Member will be told of the hearing at least fifteen days before it. Members may write to the Board before the hearing or come to it; the Board sets a reasonable time limit for Members’ comments. You may come and speak. The Board makes no decision until the hearing ends. After the hearing the Board will grant or deny your request, in its sole discretion, and may add conditions. If it grants the request, it will tell you in writing within thirty days of its decision. The forty-five days are counted from {RECEIVED}: the hearing will be held by {HEARING_BY}.

Reading (label: the association’s plain-words note, not the document): the sentence “the Board will deny it and tell you in writing within thirty days of its decision” states 4.20(a); “every Member will be told” states 4.20(b) (“Notice shall be given to all Members”).

Who decides: the Board, “in its sole discretion” (4.20, 4.20(a), 4.20(c)), after the hearing. How to reconsider: **the documents state no reconsideration of a variance.** If the variance concerns a physical change that needs approval (CC&Rs 5.1), a disapproval is also a decision under CIV 4765 and the Owner is entitled to reconsideration at an open meeting of the Board (4765(a)(5)); the written decision then gives the reasons and the procedure (4765(a)(4)). Otherwise the Owner may ask the Board in writing to reconsider and may ask to meet and confer (CIV 5900 to 5920) (lead 3).

## 11. Handler and procedure

- **Handler:** `@handler("ccrs#4.20", role=Role.FORM_RETURN, form="variance-request", procedure="variance-request", channels=(PAYHOA, GMAIL, MAIL))` ([arrivals-design.md](../../../docs/arrivals-design.md); the documents’ section is the key because no statute is). `accepts` takes an arrival with this form’s marker (`VR`), a PayHOA form id, or a request classified `ResponseKind.VARIANCE`. `read` is evidence only. `plan` stamps the date received, opens the clocks of section 7 as schedule occurrences (the 45-day hearing; the day the notice to Members must go, 15 days before the hearing), drafts the acknowledgment, puts the request on the board packet as an item for the initial determination, and, if the Board decides to hear it, drafts the notice of the hearing for the Board to approve and a person to send (the notice to Members is a general notice; `jason notices`). It writes nothing outside jason and never decides.
- **Procedure:** `variance-request` does not exist in `src/jason/community/procedures.py`; the nearest is `respond`. Add it, with steps: (1) stamp the date received and acknowledge; (2) check the request is a restriction of Article 4 and put it on the next board agenda for the initial determination; (3) a denial on its face is sent within thirty days of the decision; (4) otherwise set the hearing within forty-five days and compute the last day for the notice to Members (fifteen days before); (5) send the notice, with the Board’s time limit for comments; (6) the Board hears, then decides in its sole discretion, with conditions if any; (7) the notice of a grant goes within thirty days of the decision; (8) record the decision, its conditions, and its scope and duration; (9) write each failure as a lesson. A variance that is a physical change also starts the architectural application of [improvement-requests.md](../improvement-requests.md).
- **jason never:** decides, or recommends a decision on, a variance; sets the hearing without the Board; or sends the notice to Members on its own initiative (AGENTS.md, Boundaries).

## 12. Edge cases

- **Co-owners.** An application may be signed by one co-owner on behalf of the others or by all; the date received is the first receipt. The Board decides for the Unit.
- **A representative.** A person with a power of attorney or other written authority may sign for the Owner, with the authority attached; the application is the Owner’s.
- **A tenant or a non-owner.** Not an Owner; a tenant who writes “I want a variance” is told the owner applies, and the association does not act on it.
- **A variance that is really a physical change.** A change to the exterior is also an application under CC&Rs 5.1 and B-18; the form carries both and the clocks run beside each other ([improvement-requests.md](../improvement-requests.md)).
- **A variance that is really a rental exception.** 4.15(c) speaks to a hardship or limited-term waiver of 4.15; 4.20 reaches “the provisions of Article 4”, and 4.15 is in Article 4. The Board decides which route applies; the form is accepted either way and the handler says which is read (lead 6).
- **A request about a disability.** Read as a request for a reasonable accommodation as well; never refused for being on this form ([the accommodation page](../../../docs/form-templates/accommodation-request.md)).
- **A request that arrives without the form.** A letter or email that asks the Board to excuse the Owner from a restriction is a written application. The handler reads it into the form, asks once for what is missing, and counts the 45 days from the day it arrived.
- **Language and accessibility.** The documents’ words stay in English; large print, another format, a person to read the form aloud or write it down, and a reasonable accommodation are offered.
- **A request to withdraw.** The Owner may withdraw at any time before the Board decides; the hearing’s notice is withdrawn and Members are told.

## 13. Leads for the board and counsel

These are readings, not rules, and jason resolves none. Each should be read in full by a person before anyone acts on it.

1. **No time for the initial determination, and no consequence for a missed hearing.** 4.20(a) states only the thirty days to notify a denial; (b) fixes the forty-five days to hold the hearing; neither says what follows if the hearing is late. The proposed twenty-one days (section 7, row 2) only keeps the dates the document fixes in order. The board adopts a written policy.
2. **A denial after the hearing.** 4.20(c) states the thirty days for notifying a grant. It states no time and no form for a denial after the hearing. A variance that is a physical change must be decided in writing with reasons (CIV 4765(a)(4)); the proposal gives every denial the same thirty days and a written reason.
3. **Is there reconsideration?** The documents state none for a variance; 4.15(g) says “final and conclusive” for a rental application, and 4.20 does not. For a physical change the statute gives reconsideration at an open meeting (4765(a)(5)). Reading (labeled; the profile’s inventory, lead 4): the 4.20 hearing and decision are the first decision, and reconsideration under 4765(a)(5) follows a disapproval. For any other variance whether an Owner may ask for reconsideration, and whether the decision is subject to meet and confer (CIV 5900 to 5920), is for counsel.
4. **A hearing is not a discipline hearing.** 4.20(b) requires a hearing with fifteen days’ notice to all Members; it is a different procedure from the hearing before discipline (CIV 5855; Enforcement Policy, “Due Process Requirements”). The two are not mixed on the form.
5. **“Sole discretion” and good faith.** 4.20 says the Board decides “in its sole discretion”. For a physical change, CIV 4765(a)(2) says a decision “may not be unreasonable, arbitrary, or capricious”. Both can be obeyed: the Board alone decides, and decides in good faith ([improvement-requests.md](../improvement-requests.md), the same reading for 5.1). Noted, not a `Conflict` row.
6. **4.20 and 4.15(c).** 4.15(c) lets the Board waive 4.15 for hardship or a limited term; 4.20 lets it grant a variance from any Article 4 provision, 4.15 included. Two readings remain: (a) 4.15(c) is the exclusive route for 4.15 because it speaks to it specifically; (b) 4.20 is also available. The documents do not say. The board asks counsel.
7. **The notice to all Members.** The document says “Notice shall be given to all Members” and no more: not the method, not whether it names the Owner or the Unit, not what it says. The form lets the Owner say how it reads (`notice-text`); the Board writes it. Whether it is a general notice (CIV 4045), and whether naming the Owner is a privacy matter for an Owner who gives health or money as a ground, is for the board and counsel.
8. **A hearing as a board meeting.** If the hearing is a board meeting, CIV 4920 and 4930 apply: notice by general delivery with the agenda, and no action on an item not on it. The fifteen days of 4.20(b) are longer than 4920’s four, and control. A variance is not a ground for executive session in CIV 4935(a); the hearing is open.
9. **The Owner’s Manual’s parking permit cites 4.20.** B-12(o) says the board “shall adopt a parking permit program ... (CC&Rs §4.20)”. 4.20 is Variances; the parking provisions are 4.11. Whether each permit is a variance under 4.20 (so each would need a hearing and notice to all Members) or a rule under 4.11(g) is open ([parking-permit.md](parking-permit.md), lead 2).
10. **Time limit for comments.** 4.20(b) says the Board “shall establish a reasonable time limit for Member comments during the hearing”. None is on file in jason’s documents. CIV 4925(b) requires the board to establish a limit for speaking at any meeting; one limit may serve both. For the board.
11. **A fee.** No fee is listed for a variance. The Enforcement Policy lists “Administrative Fee - Other $25” and “Administrative Fee - Hearing $50” beside the discipline fines; they are not read as applying to a variance. Whether a fee may be charged for an application the Owner makes is for the board and counsel.

**The statutory (here, documents’) checklist test, in words.** (1) The form is a written application from an Owner and carries the restriction of Article 4, the specific application, and the ground in the document’s two sets of words. (2) The form states the forty-five days, the fifteen days, the Board’s time limit for Member comments, the thirty days for a denial on its face, and the thirty days for a grant. (3) The form asks no signature of neighbors and no finances or health. (4) The recital block contains the exact words of 4.20 and the exact words of CIV 4765(a)(4) and (5) and 4925(b), each filled from the document or the shelf, and the build fails if a section is gone. (5) The acknowledgment carries `{RECEIVED}`, `{HEARING_BY}`, `{DECIDER}`, `{REFERENCE}`. (6) The clocks of rows 2, 6, and 10 are labeled PROPOSED POLICY; the others cite the section that sets them. (7) The same template renders paper, PDF, email, and PayHOA with the same questions in the same order, each carrying `VR`. (8) A made-up return of each fixture reads back to the same answers after a bad scan.
