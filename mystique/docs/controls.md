# Controls jason suggests from the Bylaws

What [docs/controls.md](../../docs/controls.md) would suggest to this association's board from its Bylaws, read in [officer-duties.md](officer-duties.md). Every suggestion is the board's to adopt, decline, or defer; jason adopts none. A suggestion shows the provision whole (quoted there), the floor it sets, and a reading labeled as one. No amount is suggested: a threshold is the board's number.

| # | Family | The provision and its floor | What jason would suggest above the floor (a reading) | Enforced at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payment signers | 12.9: signed by "any two Directors," "any two officers," or "any officer ... and any Director," or "in any other manner specified by the Board of Directors" | Name the manner: for example the Treasurer and one other director for payments above an amount the board sets; any two directors below it. 12.9(iv) lets the board specify it | the approvals engine, money kinds (`payhoa-attach-bills` and the payment plans) |
| 2 | Reserve withdrawals | 12.9: "at least two persons who shall be members of the Board of Directors or one member of the Board of Directors and one officer who is not a member of the Board" | Keep the floor and name the two (not the manager's staff); show the second signature on the reserve plan | the approvals engine, reserve transfers |
| 3 | Contract authorization | 8.3: "Unless expressly authorized by resolution of the Board, no officer shall have any power or authority to bind the Association" | A resolution on record before an officer signs a contract; the contract's term and size are checked against 8.17 (one year; five percent of budgeted gross expenses) as a warning | the contract-notice and improvement-request runs |
| 4 | Limits of the board's powers | 8.17: no contract over one year (with listed exceptions), capital improvements over five percent of budgeted gross expenses, a sale over five percent, compensation to directors or officers, entry into a unit in a non-emergency without 24 hours' written notice, unless the members consent | A check on a plan or a contract that would pass a limit, naming the section; refusal is the board's choice | contract and spend plans |
| 5 | Review cadence | 9.6 sets a quarterly minimum; Civil Code 5500 requires the same reviews monthly unless the documents are more stringent (Conflict row `bylaws-quarterly-review`) | Monthly review by each director, ratified at the next meeting, recorded by the Secretary | the monthly-review run |
| 6 | Manager's authority | 8.6: the board may engage a manager "and ... prescribe their duties" | A spending amount above which the manager asks, and whom; the Bylaws state none, so it is the board's number | bill attach, plans |
| 7 | Delivery class | Civil Code 4040(a)(2) for an individual notice; the Bylaws name no class | A notice letter defaults to first class (done, 2026-10-11) | the Mailroom |
| 8 | Recorder | 10.10: the Secretary keeps the minutes book and the record of giving notice | The Secretary records decisions, minutes, and that a notice was given; the Vice-President records only while the President is noted absent or disabled | Decisions, notices, meetings |
| 9 | Vice-President acting | 10.9: acts "in the absence or disability of the President" | A recorded absence (the Secretary notes it in the minutes) before the Vice-President's presiding or signing as President is live | office views, meetings |

The roster rows carry the offices: the Bylaws call the Chief Financial Officer the Treasurer ("may from time to time be referred to as the Treasurer," 10.1) and the President "the chief executive officer" (10.8); the profile treats the CFO as the Treasurer and the CEO as the President (confirmed by the Treasurer-Secretary, 2026-10-11).
