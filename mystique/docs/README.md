# Mystique: the instance reference

This folder is the `mystique` profile's own documentation: this association's setup, which the general docs (`docs/`) describe without naming. Findings that name owners, parties, matters, or figures stay in `mystique/notes/` (not checked in). See `docs/profiles.md`.

- [vendor-portals.md](vendor-portals.md): the pest control vendor and its FieldPortals account.
- [filing-by-kind.md](filing-by-kind.md): where each kind of attachment is filed in the Drive, the library paths that match, and who chose them.
- [backflow-program.md](backflow-program.md): the six backflow assemblies, the City account, and each year's notices, tests, and repairs.
- [life-safety-watchlist.md](life-safety-watchlist.md): each life-safety inspection and record, where it stands for the association, and what is on file.
- [responses.md](responses.md): what the association's members' requests held, and how well their kinds are read.
- [manual.md](manual.md): the Owner's Manual and Rules taken apart: its parts, the classification and its questions, the books, the official rules and their adoption history, the generated manual, and what the board decides.
- [rule-citations.md](rule-citations.md): how this association's rule documents number their sections, which numbers more than one document prints, the parts of the manual, and what the scoping reads for this profile (general: [docs/rule-citations.md](../../docs/rule-citations.md)).
- [rule-authority.md](rule-authority.md): where the governing documents give the board power to make rules, by subject, and which subjects have rules on file (general: [docs/rule-authority.md](../../docs/rule-authority.md)).
- [document-segments.md](document-segments.md): the owner's manual's parts as the segmentation reads them, the archive scanned in batches, and how the gold labels are kept.
- [living.md](living.md): the living CC&Rs' readings: the OCR re-read trial and its numbers.
- [standard-forms.md](standard-forms.md): the provisions that imply a form here (rental application, variance, home improvement, registration, permits, payment plan, records), with their clocks, and the leads for the board and counsel.
- [form-templates/rental-application.md](form-templates/rental-application.md): the rental application, the exception request, and the rehearing request (CC&Rs 4.15): the words, the form, the clocks, the register, and the leads. Held: designed, not offered, until the board takes up `rental-approvals-4-15`.
- [form-templates/variance-request.md](form-templates/variance-request.md): the variance request (CC&Rs 4.20): the three steps, the 45-day hearing and 15-day notice, and the gaps the document leaves.
- [form-templates/resident-registration.md](form-templates/resident-registration.md): the annual resident and vehicle registration (Owner's Manual B-1): the form, the register, the 30 days, and the fine's leads. Waits on the same board decision.
- [form-templates/parking-permit.md](form-templates/parking-permit.md): the parking permit, guest permits, replacement, and a longer guest stay (Owner's Manual B-12(o), (b); CC&Rs 4.11): the two prices and what the program rests on.
- [improvement-requests.md](improvement-requests.md): home improvement requests mapped to the association's provisions, its forms, the clocks against the meeting schedule, and the gaps where a written policy is needed.
- [mold-program.md](mold-program.md): the declaration's mold inspection and prevention program: the requirement step by step, the adoption on record, the inspections done against the quarterly clock, the owners' part, proposed obligations, and the program's outline for the board.
- [maintenance-manual.md](maintenance-manual.md): the declaration's inspection and maintenance manual: the requirement, what the records hold, the review by component, proposed obligations, and the manual's outline for the board.
- [structure-recovery.md](structure-recovery.md): counts from running the structure-recovery benchmark on the owner's manual (general: [docs/structure-recovery.md](../../docs/structure-recovery.md)).
