## Built and first conveyance

The assessor's year built differs by parcel block: one block of two buildings is 2008, the rest 2020 to 2022. The painting groups
above mix those vintages. That is worth asking the preparer about, because a seven-year cycle counted from 2008 and one counted
from 2020 do not land on the same years. The parcel-to-building bridge is missing locally, so year built cannot yet be rolled up by
building. First conveyance per building is not computed (the existing first-conveyances report is superseded).

## What a photo shows (3031 Mesmerizing Walk, building 6, October 3, 2026)

Read by eye, not measured. The entry has a light warm stucco field, white window frames and door surround, a dark brown entry door,
and dark brown fascia under the eave. That fits the schedule's trim (white), entry doors, and fascia rows, and a field in scheme 1
or 2 (a light gray or an off-white; the two are hard to tell apart in one photo). The neighboring bay on the left is a muted green
stucco, which would be scheme 3's field. So schemes alternate by bay within a building, and the scheme is per unit, not per building.

Two cautions. The green looks grayer and darker than the schedule's olive yellow-green; shade, white balance, or a repaint could
explain it, so it needs a check against a color fan deck on site. And sampling pixels from the saved copy of the photo gave a strong
blue cast (its white trim measured blue), so no color match was made from numbers. Not on the schedule: the black wrought-iron patio
fence and gate, light fixtures, and mechanical screens. Garage doors are not visible from the front.

## Interior colors: what is on file

- **Declaration 7.5 (Interior Decorations)** gives each owner "complete discretion as to furniture, furnishings, and interior
  decorating" and "the exclusive right to paint ... the interior surfaces of the walls, ceilings, floors, and doors bounding his or
  her Unit," except as limited by 4.18, which concerns sound transmission and soft-cover floors on lower levels, not color. 7.4(a)
  makes the owner maintain the interior's finished surfaces. The Owner's Manual (2 Interior Home) repeats the owner's responsibility.
  Read together, the association does not prescribe interior colors (a reading; the quoted words govern).
- **No interior color record found.** The library has no finish, interior, or color-selection document, and no embedded text names
  an interior color. The developer's interior packages (the developer and builders are in the specification) are the likely source.
- **A lead, not read:** a 2026-05-15 Gmail thread with the subject "Interior Paint Colors" from a realty domain. Its body was not read.
- **Already answered to owners:** the Owner's Manual has "Is touch up paint available?": the association paints exteriors about every 7
  years, the painters may leave excess paint, and an owner can order from Sherwin-Williams by the exterior schedule (the example is a
  quart of SW 7545 for an entry door). A 2025 maintenance request about peeling door frames was answered the same way: the declaration
  makes doors the owner's responsibility (7.2), but the association paints the whole building, doors included, every 5 to 7 years.
